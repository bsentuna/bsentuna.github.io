Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

  • There is no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • We use Google Firebase to see how the game is played and to fix crashes, and Google AdMob to show ads. These services use device identifiers.
  • Payments are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your card details.
  • You can change your ad and analytics choices at any time in the game's Settings.

1. Who we are

Maki Meow ("the game") is made by Burak Şentuna, an independent developer based in Türkiye ("we", "us"). We decide how the information described here is used, which makes us the data controller under the EU and UK GDPR and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

Contact: dev.makimeow@gmail.com

2. What we collect and why

Saved only on your device

Your level, coins, boosters, lives, restaurant progress, settings and a random player number (shown at the bottom of Settings). This stays on your phone and is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it.

Gameplay statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

Levels started, won and lost, boosters used, items bought, session start and length, game version, device model, operating system version, language, approximate country (worked out from your IP address; we do not store the IP address) and an app-instance identifier created by Firebase. The random player number may be attached so that we can find your data if you ask us to.

Why: to balance level difficulty, find places where players get stuck and improve the game.

Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

When the game crashes: the technical state at that moment (which code failed, device model, operating system version, free memory, game version) and a crash-reporting installation identifier.

Why: to find and fix bugs.

Ads (Google AdMob and its advertising partners)

Your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone only if you allow tracking), IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

Why: to show the ads that keep the game free, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ads and prevent fraud. Ads are personalised only where you have agreed to it.

Purchases

Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) process all payments. We receive a confirmation of which item was bought and an order number, so that we can deliver the item and restore it later. We never receive your card or billing details.

Support messages

If you e-mail us, we receive your e-mail address and whatever you write, and use them only to answer you.

Game engine

The game is built with Unity. Unity Technologies may receive limited technical information (such as device model and operating system) as part of running the engine.

3. Legal bases

  • Providing the game and delivering purchases: performance of our contract with you.
  • Crash reports, gameplay statistics and non-personalised ads: our legitimate interest in running, fixing, improving and funding the game. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first.
  • Personalised ads: your consent.
  • Answering support messages: our legitimate interest in helping you.
  • Keeping purchase records: our legal obligations, such as tax law.

4. Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the first time you open the game, you are asked whether you agree to analytics and personalised ads. Nothing starts before you choose. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • iPhone: the game asks before it tracks you across other companies' apps. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings (for example Settings → Google → All services → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • If you say no to personalised ads, you still see ads, but they are less relevant to you.
  • Buying "No Ads" removes the ads shown between levels. Reward videos that you choose to watch stay available.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that run parts of the game for us:

  • Google LLC (Firebase and AdMob) and the advertising partners AdMob works with: Google Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, Firebase privacy
  • Apple and Google, for purchases: Apple Privacy Policy
  • Unity Technologies: Unity Privacy Policy

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

6. International transfers

These providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States. They protect it with safeguards the law accepts, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep it

  • Data on your device: until you delete the game.
  • Gameplay statistics: up to 14 months.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Support e-mails: as long as needed to help you, at most 2 years.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax law requires.

8. Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or under the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has used the game and you want their information deleted, write to us.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, send it to you in a portable form, or withdraw your consent. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

We do not know who you are, so please include the player number shown at the bottom of the game's Settings when you write to us. It helps us find your data. Write to dev.makimeow@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

10. Security

We do not run servers of our own. The providers above send information over encrypted connections and protect it with their own security measures.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. If the change is important, we also tell you in the game.

Çökme raporları (Google Firebase Crashlytics)
